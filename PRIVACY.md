# WaveNest Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

WaveNest is a Chrome extension for playing radio stations and podcast episodes. WaveNest does not sell personal information, run advertising, create user accounts, or send analytics to the developer.

## Data stored on your device

WaveNest stores playback preferences, volume, favorites, cached station and podcast metadata, playback position, and recent playback-error information in Chrome extension storage. This information is used only to provide the player, restore preferences, and improve navigation. It remains in your Chrome profile and can be removed by uninstalling the extension or clearing its extension data.

## Network requests

WaveNest contacts the radio, podcast, and catalog providers listed in the extension manifest to retrieve catalogs, RSS metadata, audio streams, and artwork requested by the user. Those third-party providers may receive standard connection information such as an IP address and browser user agent under their own privacy policies. WaveNest does not route these requests through a developer-operated server.

## Data sharing

The developer does not collect, retain, sell, or share personal or sensitive user data. WaveNest does not use extension data for credit decisions, advertising, or any purpose unrelated to its single purpose of finding and playing audio.

## Security

WaveNest uses HTTPS for its bundled catalogs and streams. Extension code is packaged locally and does not execute remotely hosted code.

## Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue at https://github.com/Tbe24/WaveNest/issues.
